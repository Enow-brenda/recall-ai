How Recall Works

1

Connect Your Inbox Securely

We use Google OAuth to establish a secure, read-only connection to your Gmail account. Your emails are indexed locally and never shared with third parties.

2

Ask Natural Questions

Don't rely on keyword searches. Type questions exactly as you would ask a colleague, like "What were the key takeaways from the marketing sync?"

3

Find Exact Answers with Sources

Recall synthesizes information across threads and provides a concise answer, complete with direct links to the original emails for verification.
